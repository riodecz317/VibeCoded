What's persisted vs. computed

Persisted in Supabase: roster, department rules, OT entries, and every approval action (who, level, timestamp, comment).
Computed: weekly cumulative hours and risk level, via a Postgres view + function, so every client sees the same number.
Automated: a database trigger builds the 4-level approval chain the moment an entry is inserted, and a second trigger blocks approving a level before the levels below it clear — enforced in the database, not just the UI.

New OT Entry

Department Parameter Table

Edits write directly to department_rules. Every open risk calculation re-reads this table, so changes take effect on the next entry or refresh.

Pending across all entries

Every approval row currently waiting on someone, database-wide. Approve/Reject writes to approvals as the person set in "Acting as" above. A level can't be approved until the levels below it are cleared — enforced by a Postgres trigger, not just this UI.

Bulk roster upload

Upload a CSV/XLSX to add or update many employees in one call. Columns: EmployeeID, Name, Dept, Grade, TL, SM, OM, BUH. New departments get a default rule row (Max 10h/wk, High-Risk 15h) — adjust it on the Rule Engine tab after.

Bulk OT entries upload

Upload many entries in a single batched insert — one round trip instead of one per row, so a whole week for a whole team lands in seconds. Columns: EmployeeID, Date, Hours, Reason, Handover (Handover optional; auto-filled when Hours > 4 and left blank). Rows with an unrecognized Employee ID, a future date, or zero/blank hours are skipped and listed below — nothing else in the batch is affected.
